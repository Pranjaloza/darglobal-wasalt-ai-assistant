DarGlobal Today
DarGlobal PLC is an international real estate developer specializing in exclusive luxury developments across the world’s most coveted cosmopolitan cities.

Catering to global citizens seeking prime investments, second homes, and luxury living, DarGlobal transforms real estate into economic catalysts, unlocking opportunities for both investors and the countries it enters.

DarGlobal has partnered with over 11 world-renowned luxury brands, including Trump Organization, Aston Martin, Automobili Lamborghini, ELIE SAAB, Marriott Residences, Missoni, Mouawad, Fendi, Pagani Automobili, Paramount and W Hotels. These collaborations offer unique investment opportunities in global cities, providing both wealth growth and protection.

Listed on the London Stock Exchange, DarGlobal goes beyond property development; it drives economic transformation by attracting international clients and Foreign Direct Investment (FDI). With a presence in 13 international cities across 8 countries, DarGlobal’s portfolio spans the United Kingdom, Spain, Greece, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Oman, the United States, and Qatar. The company is also expanding into world-class hospitality, developing luxury hotels in Dubai, Muscat, Riyadh and Jeddah. To support its international clientele, DarGlobal has sales and customer service offices in New York, London, Marbella, Athens, Dubai, Jeddah, Riyadh, Muscat, and Doha.
