Why INVEST

Spain is considered one of the most attractive countries for real estate investments on the European continent, especially in the south. Along its 8,000km of coastal seafront lies Costa Del Sol, an ideal location for those looking for a vacation home, a place to retire, or open a new business. Its privileged geographic location is ideal for living and international business, as it connects Europe, South America and the African continent. All with the benefit of a golden visa in the heart of the EU.

Location

Tierra Viva is located in the heart of Benahavís, the jewel of Costa Del Sol, and just 15 min away from Marbella.     Frequently Asked Questions

    Tierra Viva is a limited collection of ultra-luxury gated villas in Benahavís, Spain, developed by DarGlobal with design inspired by Automobili Lamborghini. Villas are staggered across the hillside at different levels in the Los Jaralillos district to ensure every home captures unobstructed panoramas of the Mediterranean Sea.

    Automobili Lamborghini provided the design inspiration for Tierra Viva, with the project integrating the brand's approach to cutting-edge design, technology, and artisanal craftsmanship into villa architecture and interiors. The collaboration between DarGlobal and Lamborghini positions Tierra Viva as a convergence of performance culture and ultra-luxury residential living.

    Tierra Viva offers a limited collection of villas ranging from 700 to 1,450 sqm. The collection is available in different villa configurations, including select units with an in-villa car lift. Contact DarGlobal for current unit availability and full specification details.

    Tierra Viva is under development with completion scheduled for June 2028. Construction is actively underway, with progress updates available on the DarGlobal website. Contact the sales team for current payment plan terms and unit availability.

    Tierra Viva is in the hills of Los Jaralillos, Benahavís, 15 minutes from Marbella and within easy reach of Puerto Banús and Sotogrande. The location gives direct access to 12 nearby golf clubs, Andalusian villages, and the shores of the Mediterranean, while remaining in one of the most prestigious residential areas on the Costa Del Sol.

    Tierra Viva is a gated community with 24/7 premium security, an exclusive residents' clubhouse, sauna, steam room, and private pool areas. Select Painite villas include an in-villa car lift. The community is designed around the concept of seclusion and belonging rather than shared hotel-style facilities.

    Spain's Golden Visa programme is available to non-EU buyers making qualifying real estate investments. Given villa sizes and values at Tierra Viva, buyers should confirm current programme thresholds and eligibility with a qualified legal adviser, as Spain's Golden Visa rules have been subject to legislative review. DarGlobal can assist with introductions to legal professionals.

    Spain is consistently ranked as offering among the highest real estate ROI in Europe, with low Eurozone interest rates between 3 and 5%, open regulations for foreign investors, and a robust tourism sector that supports rental returns. Benahavís specifically is one of the wealthiest municipalities in Spain and benefits from year-round Mediterranean climate and high-speed train connections to the wider coast.

    Branded residences at this tier consistently command significant price premiums over comparable unbranded stock. Tierra Viva combines the scarcity of a limited villa collection in Benahavís, one of Europe's most sought-after addresses, with a globally recognised automotive design brand. That pairing targets a very specific international buyer pool, which supports both value retention and resale appetite.

    Buyers can register interest, browse the collection, or download the project brochure through DarGlobal at darglobal.co.uk. The sales team can provide current villa availability, pricing, and payment plan details on request.
